Logo kit

The Webvieu logo is a “v” inside a magnifying lens, followed by the name “web” + “vieu”. All lettering is converted to shapes, so the SVG and PNG files look the same everywhere, with no font needed.

Using it well

  • Keep empty space around the logo at least as wide as the lens circle.
  • Use the icon on its own when space is tight, from 16 px upward.
  • Use the white version on dark or busy backgrounds.
  • Don't stretch, recolour, rotate or add effects to the logo.
  • Write the name in lowercase in the logo (“webvieu”) and as “Webvieu” in sentences.
